Sgt. Santos - Room 3 - Earthquake Drill (drop/cover/hold on)
============================================================

001.txt  [NOT_STARTED]  tags: [friendly, serious]
    Well done getting this far! I'm Sergeant Santos. Earthquakes are different from fires — they give no warning at all. So we practice until the right move is automatic.

002.txt  [NOT_STARTED]  tags: [instructive, serious]
    Before it even starts: stay away from windows and heavy unsecured furniture — and never run outside or use an elevator mid-shake.

003.txt  [NOT_STARTED]  tags: [instructive, calm]
    See that glowing table? That's your cover. Remember DROP (hold Shift) — COVER (walk under it with W) — HOLD ON until the shaking fully stops.

004.txt  [NOT_STARTED]  tags: [reassuring, alert]
    Ready to try? The floor is about to shake — it's only practice, you're completely safe. Head for that glowing table the moment it starts!

005.txt  [PRE_DRILL]  tags: [urgent, alert]
    Any second now — keep your eyes on that glowing green table!

006.txt  [PRE_DRILL]  tags: [urgent, instructive]
    Remember: away from windows, away from anything tall and heavy — straight to the table.

007.txt  [QUAKE_ACTIVE]  tags: [urgent, commanding]
    Get under the glowing table! Hold W to walk there, then press and hold Shift to crouch — and stay put!

008.txt  [DONE]  tags: [relieved, proud]
    And... the shaking has stopped. You dropped, covered, and held on like a pro!

009.txt  [DONE]  tags: [serious, cautionary]
    Real quakes have aftershocks too — don't jump up the second it fades. You just felt why holding a bit longer matters.

010.txt  [DONE]  tags: [proud, warm]
    One last stop: follow the glowing arrow to Captain Morfe for your results. Stand tall — you've earned it. Congratulations, trainee!
