================================================================
TIPS FOR ELEVENLABS
================================================================
- Generate each line separately so they sound natural
- Use the same voice for all lines of the same character
- Keep a consistent speaking speed — not too fast for kids
- For WARNING lines (NEVER use water, electric shock, etc.)
  make the voice sound more serious and firm
- Export as MP3 then convert to OGG using Audacity or ffmpeg
  ffmpeg command: ffmpeg -i input.mp3 -c:a libvorbis output.ogg
